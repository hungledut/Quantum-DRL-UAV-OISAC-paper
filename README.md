## Quantum Deep Policy Gradient Reinforcement Learning
This is the source code of our paper [Quantum DRL-Based UAV Placement with Optical Sensing-Enabled Hybrid FSO/RF Backhaul Design](https://apwcs2026.org/assets/a2-2.pdf) accepted to IEEE VTS APWCS 2026, proposing the Quantum Deep Policy Gradient for UAV Placement in dynamic networks.

### Train our proposed Quantum Deep Policy Gradient model🤖
~~~
py QuantumDRL_MultiUAV.py
~~~
### Test our proposed Quantum Deep Policy Gradient model🌗
~~~
py QDRL_testing.py
~~~
